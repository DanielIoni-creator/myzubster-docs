## 4. Smart Contract Logic

### Plant Registration Flow

    User submits plant data (photo, GPS, species)

    AI Agent analyzes and verifies

    Fee is calculated based on plant size

    Payment is processed in XMR

    Rewards distributed to user (0.001 XMR)

    Plant recorded on blockchain

    Plant appears on global map

### Verification Flow

    Plant is submitted for verification

    AI Agent analyzes data quality

    Community votes (upvote/downvote)

    Quality score is calculated

    Status determined (verified/pending/rejected)

    Rewards distributed if verified (0.002 XMR)
